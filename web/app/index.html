Frozen research definition

How was this research produced?

Data, modeling logic, spatial provenance, and interpretation limits used by this explorer.

01

Def5 definition

Def5 is an isolated or abrupt heavy-rain pulse observed at a gauge station-hour. The definition is final and frozen.

  1. Rain(E) ≥ the station-specific wet-hour P99.
  2. Rain(E) ≥ 20 mm/h.
  3. Rain(E−1) + Rain(E−2) + Rain(E−3) ≤ 5 mm.
  4. max(Rain(E+1), Rain(E+2), Rain(E+3)) < 20 mm/h.
  5. All hours refer to the same station and exact consecutive-hour context.
  6. Every required rainfall value must be finite and nonmissing.

Missing or structurally absent rainfall is never replaced by zero. Official Def5 for 2012–2025 contains 1,123 station-hours.

02

The modeling unit is one station-hour: predictors at time t forecast Target_Def5_tPlus1 at t+1 hour.

05

Spatial data and coverage

The explorer uses the Upper Ping Basin boundary, three administrative boundary features, 229 HydroRIVERS features, 40 station metadata points, 38 Evaluation 2025 station summaries, and 95 evaluated Def5 event points. Spatial source layers were converted from their recorded CRS to EPSG:4326 for web display.

ERA5 station-hour predictors use the nearest native grid cell, not spatial interpolation; ERA5 time was converted from UTC to Asia/Bangkok (ICT) before joining.

Gauge-observed localization does not establish storm-cell radius, storm footprint, physical storm size, or absence of rain between stations.

06

Model evaluation

Average Precision and ROC AUC measure ranking behavior under a rare outcome. ROC AUC 0.86402726 must not be described as 86.4% accuracy. At threshold 0.5, rows are Actual and columns are Predicted: TP 71, FN 24, FP 24,193, TN 97,008.

The very low prevalence—0.07832%—is central to interpreting precision and the high false-positive count.

07

SHAP and weighted KDE

Station-level SHAP summarizes information the fitted model relied on. The five KDE layers visualize the spatial concentration of model attribution at observed stations for Rain_t, q_850, u_850, u10, and Temp_t.

They do not represent rainfall intensity, feature-value surfaces, predicted probability, causal effects, or physical storm extent. Every KDE retains the original 20 km bandwidth and 1 km cell size. Feature-specific stretches support comparison of spatial pattern, not direct comparison of absolute color intensity between features.

08

GIS and derived-data provenance

Scientific inputs remain read-only. Browser files are separate derivatives produced through geometry/attribute conversion, CRS reprojection, documented display simplification, nearest-neighbour KDE resampling, bilinear elevation display resampling, exact event-key matching, and the specified hourly visualization aggregation. No model was refit and no Def5 rule was changed.

09

Current limitations

  • ERA5 timestamps were converted from UTC to Asia/Bangkok (ICT, UTC+7) before station-hour joining; station-source timezone remains unverified and stored datetimes are not converted again in the website.
  • The full E−6:E+6 rainfall context is absent, so no hydrograph is displayed.
  • Satellite imagery is not connected because no authorized source was provided.
  • Interactive summaries for 2012–2024 are deferred.
  • Elevation uses a 2nd–98th percentile display stretch and subtle hillshade; source raster values are unchanged.
